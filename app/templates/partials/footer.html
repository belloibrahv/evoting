TASFUED EVS

A secure, cryptographically verifiable electronic voting system for Tai Solarin Federal University of Education student union elections.

Ballots are encrypted with RSA-2048 OAEP and protected by SHA-256 integrity hashing. Your vote is secret — even the system cannot read it.
